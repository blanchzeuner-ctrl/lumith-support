Common questions

How do I restrict distracting apps?

Grant Screen Time access when prompted, then select apps, categories, or websites using Apple’s picker. Set a duration and start a session, or save and enable a repeating plan.

Do I need to allow the microphone?

Only if you choose to read a commitment aloud. You can use text confirmation instead. When available, recognition runs on your device. If it is unavailable, using Apple’s speech service requires your explicit consent. Lumith does not save recordings or transcripts.

How do gems grow?

A completed focus session adds growth to the gem you are cultivating. Ending a session early saves actual focus time but does not award the completion gem. Some gem types require Pro.

Why has a plan not started, or a restriction not cleared?

Check the enabled plan’s time, repeat days, selected apps, and Screen Time authorization. Reopen Lumith to refresh the current session. If the issue persists, contact us with the expected start or end time and whether your phone was locked.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

Use Restore Purchases in Lumith with the Apple Account used to subscribe. Pro offers monthly and annual subscriptions. To manage or cancel one, open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Uninstalling Lumith does not cancel a subscription. Trial eligibility and current pricing appear on Apple’s purchase screen.

Where are my focus records stored?

Focus records and gem progress are stored locally on your device. The current app does not offer developer-operated cloud sync. See the Privacy Policy for details about local storage, Apple services, and deletion.
